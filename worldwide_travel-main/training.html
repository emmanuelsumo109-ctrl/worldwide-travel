About The Institute

Invest in Your Career

The Worldwide Travel Training Institute was launched in 2021 to address a growing demand for skilled professionals in Liberia's travel and hospitality sector. Our programs are designed to equip students with practical, industry-relevant skills through both certificate and diploma pathways.

Whether you're entering the travel industry for the first time or looking to upgrade your skills, our courses offer hands-on learning, experienced instructors, and direct connections to career opportunities through our agency network.
