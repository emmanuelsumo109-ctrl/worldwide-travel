Program Focus

Learn The Operational Side of Travel

Airline ticketing is one of the most practical entry points into the travel industry. Our training is designed for people who want to understand reservation workflows, customer handling, itinerary logic, and the day-to-day structure of travel agency work.

Whether your goal is employment, career transition, or stronger travel industry knowledge, this training gives you a more practical foundation than surface-level theory alone.

What You Will Learn

Reservation Workflows

Understand how bookings are structured, modified, reviewed, and communicated in a professional setting.

  • Passenger booking logic
  • Route and itinerary organization
  • Travel record handling
  • Booking accuracy and communication

Ticketing Fundamentals

Build the practical knowledge needed to understand ticketing processes and airline service expectations.

  • Fare awareness and booking basics
  • Baggage and travel rule interpretation
  • Travel timing and confirmation flow
  • Customer-facing support standards
